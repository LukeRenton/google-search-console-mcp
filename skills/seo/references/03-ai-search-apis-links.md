# Mined domain knowledge — claude-seo skills (modern/AI-era content)

## AI search & citability (GEO)

- Google's official position (AI optimization guide, announced 2026-05-15, updated 2026-06-29): optimizing for generative AI search "is still SEO"; AEO/GEO are rebranded labels for the same work. AI Overviews and AI Mode are grounded in the same ranking/quality systems as classic Search, with two layers on top: RAG/grounding and "query fan-out" (multiple related sub-queries pulled in before answering). (google-ai-optimization-guide.md) [VERIFY]
- Eligibility floor for any Google AI feature: the page must be indexed AND eligible to be shown with a snippet in normal Google Search — there is no separate "AI index". (google-ai-optimization-guide.md) [VERIFY]
- Google's explicit myth-busting list — you do NOT need to: create llms.txt or AI-specific markup files; "chunk" content into small pieces for AI; rewrite content with AI-specific phrasings/long-tail variations; chase inauthentic mentions across blogs/forums/videos; over-invest in structured data specifically for AI features. What matters instead: unique, non-commodity, first-hand content (their example contrasts a generic "7 Tips for First-Time Homebuyers" with a lived-experience inspection story). (google-ai-optimization-guide.md) [VERIFY]
- There is no AI-specific opt-out file for Google's AI features: appearance in AI Overviews/AI Mode is governed by the standard preview/index controls — `nosnippet`, `data-nosnippet`, `max-snippet`, `noindex` (distinct from third-party AI-crawler robots.txt controls). (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Optimal passage length for AI citation: 134–167 words, structured as self-contained answer blocks; put a direct answer in the first 40–60 words of a section. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- ~44% of AI citations come from the first 30% of a page (SE Ranking study) — front-load the most citable, self-contained answer. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- 92% of AI Overview citations come from top-10 ranking pages, but 47% come from pages ranking below position 5 — AI selection logic overlaps with but differs from classic ranking. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Recency effect: content under 3 months old is ~3x more likely to be cited in AI answers; pages left stale 6+ months lose citation eligibility (SE Ranking, 1.3M-citation study). A scheduled content-refresh program is one of the highest-leverage GEO plays. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Content with multi-modal elements (images, video, infographics, interactive tools) sees ~156% higher AI selection rates. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Brand mentions correlate ~3x more strongly with AI visibility than backlinks (Ahrefs Dec 2025 study, 75,000 brands): YouTube mentions ~0.737 correlation (strongest), Reddit/Wikipedia high, LinkedIn moderate, Domain Rating (backlinks) only ~0.266. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Only ~11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews for the same query — platform-specific optimization is required. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Platform citation-source profiles: ChatGPT cites Wikipedia (47.9%) and Reddit (11.3%) most; Perplexity cites Reddit (46.7%) and Wikipedia; Bing Copilot draws from the Bing index (IndexNow relevant); Google AI Overviews is strongly ranking-correlated. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Google runs two distinct AI citation engines: AI Overviews (strongly ranking-correlated) vs AI Mode (weakly ranking-correlated, broader pool ~9 domains cited per query). They reach the same conclusion ~86% of the time but cite the same URLs only 13.7% of the time (Ahrefs, 540K query pairs) — treat them as separate surfaces even after Google merged the UX at I/O 2026 (2026-05-19). AI Mode reportedly runs a custom version of Gemini 2.5. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- AI crawlers do NOT execute JavaScript — server-side rendering is critical for content to be readable by AI search systems. (seo-geo/SKILL.md)
- Citability signal checklist (strong): quotable sentences with specific facts/stats; self-contained extractable blocks; "X is..." / "X refers to..." definition patterns; attributed claims; unique data points. Weak: vague statements, opinion without evidence, buried conclusions. Structure: clean H1→H2→H3, question-based headings, 2–4 sentence paragraphs, tables for comparisons, lists for steps, FAQ-format Q&A. (seo-geo/SKILL.md)
- Authority/entity plays with outsized GEO impact: original research/surveys (unique citability), Wikipedia/Wikidata presence for brand and key people, author bylines with credentials, publication + last-updated dates, sameAs entity linking across platforms, presence on Reddit/YouTube/LinkedIn. (seo-geo/SKILL.md)
- Who/How/Why content-quality test (Google's helpful-content guidance): WHO created it (bylines; author background pages required for YMYL), HOW it was created (disclose AI-assisted process where readers would ask), WHY it exists ("to help people", not "to attract search clicks"). Self-audit warning signs Google lists: writing to a target word count, entering niches with no expertise, faking publication-date freshness, mass content churn for "freshness". (google-ai-optimization-guide.md)
- YMYL topics (health, finance, safety) get extra quality weight; the Sept 2025 Quality Rater Guidelines expanded YMYL to include political/social topics. (google-ai-optimization-guide.md) [VERIFY]
- Google's third-party SEO tools doc (2026-06-05): no tool guarantees rankings; third-party tools have no access to Google's internal ranking data; Search Console is the authoritative first-party source — present third-party scores as heuristics, never as Google-internal signals. (google-ai-optimization-guide.md) [VERIFY]
- New Google AI-surface features (May 2026): "Preferred Sources" (per-user preference making a chosen domain more likely in that user's results — NOT a general ranking signal; don't promise site-wide lift), "Highly Cited" badges (earned via original primary reporting that others cite), "Community Perspectives" (elevates Reddit/forum/firsthand content), inline links + hover link previews + link carousels. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Google "Information Agents" (background topic monitoring) plus agentic booking/calling were rolling out to US users summer 2026 — agent-friendly pages (real interactive elements, accessibility tree, layout stability) now matter for actions, not only citations. AI agents interact with sites via three channels: screenshots + vision model, raw HTML/DOM, and the browser accessibility tree. (seo-geo/SKILL.md, google-ai-optimization-guide.md) [VERIFY]
- AI-content policy line: generative AI content is fine if it meets Search Essentials; it becomes spam when used to scale low-value pages (QRG §4.6.5 scaled content abuse, §4.6.6 low-effort main content). Merchant-feed enforcement: AI-generated product images must carry IPTC `DigitalSourceType: TrainedAlgorithmicMedia`; AI-generated product titles/descriptions must be labeled as AI-generated in the feed. (google-ai-optimization-guide.md) [VERIFY]

## llms.txt — evidence status

- No major AI search system currently consumes `/llms.txt`, and Google states in its own docs (2026-06-29) that Google Search ignores it — "won't harm (nor help) your visibility or rankings". Never present llms.txt as a Google ranking/citation lever. (llmstxt-evidence.md) [VERIFY]
- Supporting evidence: John Mueller called the llms.txt discovery use case "a dead end" and said (2025) "no AI system currently uses llms.txt", comparing it to meta keywords; Gary Illyes (July 2025) said Google has no plans to support it; SE Ranking's 300k-domain study (Nov 2025) found only ONE of the 50 most AI-cited domains had an llms.txt; OtterlyAI server-log audit (2025) found 0.1% of AI-bot traffic requests `/llms.txt` (84 of 62,100 requests); Anthropic/Stripe/Cloudflare/NVIDIA publish the file but none confirm their crawlers consume third-party copies. (llmstxt-evidence.md) [VERIFY]
- Where llms.txt DOES matter: AI coding agents (Cursor, Continue, Cline, Claude Code) consume it for per-library docs; Mintlify auto-generates `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` for developer-docs sites. For a developer-tooling site it is a net win; for a non-developer business site the value is purely defensive optionality at zero cost. (llmstxt-evidence.md)
- llms.txt format: root-level file with `# Title`, `> description`, `## Sections` of `- [Page title](url): description` markdown links. (seo-geo/SKILL.md)

## AI crawlers & robots controls

- Key AI crawler user-agents: GPTBot (OpenAI, ChatGPT search), OAI-SearchBot (OpenAI search), ChatGPT-User (user-triggered browsing — ignores robots.txt), ClaudeBot (Anthropic web), anthropic-ai (Claude training), PerplexityBot, CCBot (Common Crawl training data), Bytespider (ByteDance), cohere-ai, Google-Extended (Gemini/Vertex training + grounding opt-out), Google-CloudVertexBot, Google-Agent (agentic browsing / Project Mariner — user-triggered, ignores robots.txt), Google-NotebookLM (user-added sources — ignores robots.txt). (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Decision rule: allow GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot for AI-search visibility; optionally block CCBot and training-only crawlers. User-triggered fetchers (ChatGPT-User, Google-Agent, Google-NotebookLM) ignore robots.txt by design — only server-side access controls can block them. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Google's canonical crawling/robots reference migrated to developers.google.com/crawling on 2025-11-20; IP-range files moved to `/crawling/ipranges/` and `googlebot.json` was renamed `common-crawlers.json`. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Emerging bot authentication: Web Bot Auth (RFC 9421) lets bots authenticate via a `Signature-Agent` header + key directory (used by Google-Agent); reverse-DNS verification remains the fallback. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- RSL 1.0 ("Really Simple Licensing", December 2025): machine-readable AI content-licensing terms, backed by Reddit, Yahoo, Medium, Quora, Cloudflare, Akamai, Creative Commons. (seo-geo/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]

## Search Console API facts

- Search Analytics data lag: 2–3 days; retention ~16 months. (search-console-api.md)
- Search Analytics query: `rowLimit` 1–25,000 per request (default 1,000); paginate by incrementing `startRow` until fewer rows return; `dataState` accepts `final` (default), `all`, `hourly_all`. (search-console-api.md) [VERIFY: hourly_all]
- Dimensions: query, page, country, device, date, searchAppearance; types: web (default), image, video, news, discover, googleNews. The `discover` and `googleNews` types do NOT support the `query` dimension or `position` metric. (search-console-api.md)
- Country codes in Search Analytics are ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 (USA, GBR, DEU), not alpha-2. (search-console-api.md)
- Regex filters (`includingRegex`/`excludingRegex`) use RE2 syntax, max 4,096 characters. (search-console-api.md)
- Anonymized-queries trap: query-level rows omit anonymized low-volume traffic, so summing query/page rows understates site totals. For accurate totals run a separate dimensionless aggregate query; never present summed dimension rows as site totals. (search-console-api.md, seo-google/SKILL.md)
- Search Analytics rate limits: 1,200 QPM per user, 1,200 QPM per site, 40,000 QPM and 30M queries/day per project. (search-console-api.md)
- URL Inspection API limits: 2,000 queries/day and 600 QPM per site; 10M QPD and 15,000 QPM per project. (search-console-api.md)
- URL Inspection returns: verdict (PASS/FAIL/NEUTRAL/PARTIAL), coverageState, robotsTxtState (ALLOWED/DISALLOWED), indexingState (INDEXING_ALLOWED/BLOCKED_BY_META_TAG/BLOCKED_BY_HTTP_HEADER), pageFetchState (SUCCESSFUL, SOFT_404, BLOCKED_ROBOTS_TXT, NOT_FOUND, ACCESS_DENIED, SERVER_ERROR, REDIRECT_ERROR, ACCESS_FORBIDDEN, BLOCKED_4XX, INTERNAL_CRAWL_ERROR, INVALID_URL), lastCrawlTime, googleCanonical vs userCanonical, crawledAs DESKTOP/MOBILE. (search-console-api.md)
- Rich results caveat: FAQPage rich results were retired 2026-05-07 (HowTo retired 2023) — don't expect them in URL Inspection rich-results output and don't treat their disappearance as a loss. (search-console-api.md) [VERIFY]
- Sitemaps in GSC report SUBMITTED URL counts only — sitemap status is not indexation truth; use URL Inspection to confirm whether specific URLs are indexed. (seo-google/SKILL.md)
- GSC "Generative AI performance report" launched 2026-06-03: dedicated AI Overviews + AI Mode visibility view; impressions only (no clicks/CTR/position/query dimension); dimensions Pages/Countries/Devices/Dates in Pacific Time; 1,000-row limit; newest data preliminary; separate Discover gen-AI report; rolled out to a subset of properties. (seo-google/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- AI Mode traffic already rolls into the standard Performance report totals (Web search type) — clicks on external links in AI Mode and their impressions are counted normally, so you CANNOT cleanly split "classic" vs "AI" traffic from GSC totals. (seo-google/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- GSC data-reliability incident: a logging error made impressions, CTR, and average position unreliable from 2025-05-13 to 2026-04-27 (clicks unaffected); fixed forward-only with NO backfill — expect an apparent impressions drop after the fix date. (seo-google/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- GSC can expose verified TikTok/Instagram/X/YouTube accounts as individual "platform properties" (verify each separately); use them for Google Search performance only, not as platform analytics. (seo-google/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Quick-win heuristic from GSC data: queries sitting at position 4–10 with high impressions are the primary optimization targets. (seo-google/SKILL.md)
- Quota scoping matters: "per site" quotas are scoped to one GSC property; "per project" quotas are shared across every property in the GCP project; "per user" is per authenticated (service) account. (rate-limits-quotas.md)

## Comparing GSC periods honestly

- If a comparison window overlaps 2025-05-13 to 2026-04-27, clicks remain usable but impressions, CTR, and average-position trends require an explicit caution (confirmed GSC impressions logging error, no backfill). (search-console-api.md) [VERIFY]
- Only present totals as authoritative when they come from a dimensionless aggregate query (query rows drop anonymized traffic); a `--limit`-style cap on returned rows is not a cap on what the totals represent. (search-console-api.md)
- Data freshness: always note the 2–3 day lag; the newest generative-AI report data is flagged preliminary by Google. (search-console-api.md, seo-google/SKILL.md)

## Indexing API mechanics

- Indexing API is officially ONLY for pages with JobPosting or BroadcastEvent-embedded-in-VideoObject structured data. For a few ordinary URLs use URL Inspection (manual/request indexing); for many ordinary URLs use sitemaps. A `URL_UPDATED` ping only means Google may recrawl soon — no indexing benefit for other page types. Always disclose this restriction. (indexing-api.md)
- Quotas: 200 publish requests/day per project (default; increase via request form), 180 read requests/min, 380 total requests/min. Daily quota resets at midnight Pacific. Batch endpoint accepts up to 100 URLs per multipart request but each URL counts individually against the daily quota. (indexing-api.md)
- Auth: service account with scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/indexing`; the service account must be an OWNER (not just user) of the GSC property, else 403. (indexing-api.md)
- Use `URL_DELETED` only when the page permanently returns 404/410; only submit URLs with actual content changes. (indexing-api.md)
- Error handling across Google SEO APIs: on 429 honor the `Retry-After` header if present, else exponential backoff 1s/2s/4s/8s/16s with 0–500ms jitter, max 5 retries; CrUX 404 means insufficient traffic, not an auth error; GSC/GA4/Indexing 403 means missing service-account permission. (rate-limits-quotas.md)

## PageSpeed / CrUX facts

- Core Web Vitals thresholds (unchanged as of 2026-07): LCP good ≤2,500ms / poor >4,000ms; INP good ≤200ms / poor >500ms; CLS good ≤0.1 / poor >0.25; FCP good ≤1,800ms / poor >3,000ms; TTFB good ≤800ms / poor >1,800ms. (pagespeed-crux-api.md)
- INP replaced FID as a Core Web Vital on 2024-03-12; FID was removed from CrUX/PSI field tools on 2024-09-09 — never reference FID. (pagespeed-crux-api.md)
- CrUX field data = 28-day rolling window of real Chrome users; daily API updates ~04:00 UTC with ~2-day lag. CrUX History API returns up to 25 weekly collection periods (each a 28-day average ending on a Sunday), updated Mondays ~04:00 UTC. (pagespeed-crux-api.md)
- CrUX gotchas: CLS p75 is string-encoded ("0.05") — parse as float; the last histogram bin has no `end` (extends to infinity); History API returns "NaN" strings / null percentiles for ineligible periods; 404 = insufficient Chrome traffic for that URL/origin (fall back from URL-level to origin-level, then to PSI lab data); API key goes in the `X-Goog-Api-Key` header. (pagespeed-crux-api.md)
- CrUX metric changes: LCP image subpart metrics (TTFB, load delay, load duration, render delay) and `largest_contentful_paint_resource_type` added January 2025; `round_trip_time` replaced `effectiveConnectionType` in February 2025. (pagespeed-crux-api.md) [VERIFY]
- Google is migrating CrUX field data out of PSI — prefer the CrUX API directly for field data; use PSI primarily for Lighthouse lab data. (pagespeed-crux-api.md) [VERIFY]
- Rate limits: PSI v5 240 QPM / 25,000 QPD (API key, free); CrUX + CrUX History share a single 150 QPM quota per project (free, no paid increase, unlimited daily). (rate-limits-quotas.md)
- Custom Search JSON API is closed to new customers (2025). (seo-google/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]

## Backlink quality judgment

- Healthy anchor-text distribution: branded 30–50%, naked URL 15–25%, generic ("click here") 10–20%, exact-match keyword only 3–10%, partial-match 5–15%. Flag exact-match >15% as a possible link-spam signal. Per-industry exact-match ranges: SaaS 3–8%, e-commerce 5–10%, local service 5–10%, publisher 3–8%. (seo-backlinks/SKILL.md, backlink-quality.md)
- Profile-health thresholds: referring domains >100 good / <20 critical; follow ratio >60% good / <40% critical; domain diversity — no single domain should exceed 5% of the profile (>25% critical); trend — rapid decline is >20% loss per quarter. (seo-backlinks/SKILL.md)
- Auto-flag (definite spam) toxic-link patterns: domain with 10,000+ outbound links per page; domain with zero Google-indexed pages; domain registered <30 days with 100+ outbound links; identical exact-match anchor from 5+ unrelated domains; doorway pages; hacked-site (pharma/casino) injections; known PBN networks; site-wide footer/sidebar links from unrelated domains; spun auto-generated content; domains under manual Google penalty. (backlink-quality.md)
- Manual-review (likely spam) patterns: >90% outbound-link ratio; cross-language links (foreign-language domain → English content); repurposed expired/auctioned domains; pages with >50 outbound links; parked/no-traffic domains; reciprocal patterns across 10+ domains; thin Web 2.0 properties; article directories; low-quality guest-post networks; niche-irrelevant links. Monitor-only: mass social bookmarking, forum profiles, press-release syndication, coupon aggregators, generic directories, hidden anchors, cloaked pages, thin affiliate content, contextless comment links, nofollow-only domains. (backlink-quality.md)
- Link-velocity red flags: 10x normal new links in a week = possible negative SEO (investigate, prep disavow); 50%+ links lost in a month = penalty or site issue (check GSC manual actions); zero new links 3+ months = content not attracting links; all new links from one TLD or one country = coordinated network activity. (backlink-quality.md)
- Disavow decision rule — disavow only when: a manual penalty exists, clear negative-SEO attack, toxic ratio exceeds ~10% of profile, or specific PBN/link-farm domains identified. Do NOT disavow: links Google likely ignores anyway, nofollow links (already devalued), legitimate low-authority sites, or small spam volumes (<2% of profile). Disavow file uses `domain:example.com` lines. (backlink-quality.md)
- Excessive links from a single domain (>50 backlinks from one domain) and links from thin pages (<100 words) are medium-risk review items, not auto-toxic. (seo-backlinks/SKILL.md)
- Referring-domain count matters more than raw backlink count; the top 50–100 referring domains capture the majority of link authority. (free-backlink-sources.md)
- Gap-analysis rule: domains linking to a competitor but not to you = the actionable link-building opportunity list; domains linking to both = relationships to maintain; 404 pages on your own site that hold backlinks = redirect targets to reclaim link equity; pages with zero backlinks = internal-linking candidates. (seo-backlinks/SKILL.md)
- Honesty rules for backlink reporting: never report a link on a JS-rendered page as "removed" (mark unverifiable); distinguish "not crawled" vs "below threshold" vs "error" for every not-found; don't emit a numeric health score from insufficient data (their gate: fewer than 4 of 7 factors = report INSUFFICIENT DATA — a low score from missing data misleads). (seo-backlinks/SKILL.md)
- Free backlink-data reality: commercial tools index ~35–45 trillion links across 500M+ referring domains; free sources combined capture ~20–40% of raw link data but ~60–70% of actionable intelligence (high-authority links surface in free samples); for sites with <500 backlinks free sources can cover 50%+ of the meaningful profile. Free-data biases: popularity bias, truncation (100–1,000-link caps), own-site-only restrictions (GSC/AWT), missing quality metrics, monthly-at-best freshness. (free-backlink-sources.md)
- Free source specifics: Moz API free tier = 2,500 rows/month at 1 request per 10s (~70% coverage for DA/PA, 3-day lag, Spam Score range 1–17%); Bing Webmaster = verified sites only, ~15% of web (Bing index), no authority metrics; Common Crawl web graph = free public domain-level graph (PageRank, harmonic centrality) updated quarterly, no anchor text or page-level data; direct verification crawling of known links is the highest-confidence check (binary exists/lost/moved). (free-backlink-sources.md)

## Programmatic / thin content

- Google's Scaled Content Abuse policy (introduced March 2024) escalated in 2025: June 2025 wave of manual actions against sites with AI-generated content at scale; August 2025 community reports of stronger SpamBrain detection of AI link schemes/content farms; Google reported a 45% reduction in low-quality unoriginal content post-March-2024 enforcement. (seo-programmatic/SKILL.md) [VERIFY]
- Site reputation abuse ("parasite SEO") language was clarified by Google on 2024-11-19 — treat third-party/hosted programmatic content as a policy risk. (seo-programmatic/SKILL.md)
- Uniqueness thresholds: flag pages as thin/penalty-risk when <40% of on-page words are unique to that page (measured against all other pages in the programmatic set; shared header/footer/nav excluded, template boilerplate INCLUDED); hard-stop consideration at <30% unique; pages where >60% of content is shared template boilerplate are penalty risks. Word count <300 = review flag. (seo-programmatic/SKILL.md)
- Rollout decision rules: 100+ pages without content review = require an audit first; 500+ pages without justification = hard stop needing explicit approval; publish in batches of 50–100 pages and monitor indexing/rankings for 2–4 weeks before expanding; human-review at least a 5–10% sample; never publish 500+ programmatic pages simultaneously unreviewed. (seo-programmatic/SKILL.md)
- Standalone value test for every generated page: "Would this page be worth publishing even if no other similar pages existed?" No "mad-libs" patterns (identical text with only the city/product name swapped). (seo-programmatic/SKILL.md)
- Safe-at-scale page types: integration pages with real setup docs/API details/screenshots; template/tool pages with downloadable content; glossary entries of 200+ words with examples; product pages with unique specs/reviews; data-driven pages with unique statistics per record. Penalty-risk types: city-swap location pages, "best X for industry Y" without industry-specific value, "[competitor] alternative" pages without real comparison data, unreviewed AI-generated pages. (seo-programmatic/SKILL.md)
- Mechanics for large programmatic sets: self-referencing canonical on every page (parameter/sort/filter variants canonical to base when duplicative; manual page wins over programmatic overlap); sitemaps split at 50,000 URLs or 50MB uncompressed (protocol limit) with a sitemap index; `<lastmod>` = actual data-update time, not generation time; exclude noindexed pages from sitemaps; hub/spoke internal linking with 3–5 related links per page and ~3–5 internal links per 1,000 words; monitor GSC crawl stats above ~10k pages; monthly indexed-vs-intended count audits; merge records with insufficient data into aggregated pages instead of publishing thin singles. (seo-programmatic/SKILL.md)

## Change detection / drift triage (symptom → cause mapping)

- Critical changes that typically cause measurable traffic loss within days: structured data removed entirely (eligible rich results drop from SERPs quickly — but do not count retired FAQ/HowTo types as losses); canonical changed or removed (signals consolidate to the wrong URL; without a canonical Google guesses, often wrongly on parameterized pages); noindex accidentally added (page dropped from index within days); H1 removed; title tag removed (Google auto-generates one, often poorly); HTTP status regressed from 2xx to 4xx/5xx. (comparison-rules.md)
- Warning-level changes (investigate within a week, sometimes intentional): title or meta-description text changed — verify keywords/CTA and monitor CTR in GSC over ~2 weeks before judging; any CWV p75 metric regressed >20% vs baseline, or Lighthouse performance down 10+ points — check recent code changes and third-party scripts; OG tags removed (broken social previews); schema content modified (validate for removed properties/type changes). (comparison-rules.md)
- H1-change significance test: compare first H1 old vs new with a similarity ratio; treat <0.5 similarity (>50% different) as a significant topical change requiring keyword-strategy review. (comparison-rules.md)
- Info-level (often positive/neutral): new schema added, H2 structure changed, generic content-hash change with no other rule triggered. (comparison-rules.md)
